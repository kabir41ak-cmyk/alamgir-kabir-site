01 / Profile

Grounded in lived experience. Focused on equal participation.

I have been involved in child rights, inclusion, youth participation and community initiatives for around a decade. I began through grassroots volunteering and awareness work, and later took on roles across youth platforms, advocacy and humanitarian operations.

What guides my work

People should be able to access information, services and opportunities without being excluded by disability, gender, age, poverty or where they live. Listening to communities is essential, but listening must lead to decisions and practical change.

What I bring

Grassroots engagement, child protection awareness, youth collaboration, frontline health-service administration, registration and information processes, reporting, coordination and a rights-based understanding of accessibility.

I was born without a right hand. That experience informs how I understand accessibility and workplace accommodation, but it does not define the limits of my contribution. I value empathy, not pity; equal opportunity, not special treatment.

02 / Areas of focus

Connected issues. Shared responsibility.

These themes shape my work, learning and advocacy. They are connected in people’s everyday lives and should not be treated as separate development agendas.

Child rights & protection

Community awareness and youth engagement addressing child marriage, child labour, violence against children and children’s right to safety and participation.

Disability inclusion

Accessibility, reasonable accommodation, inclusive communication and meaningful participation of persons with disabilities in workplaces, services and decisions.

Gender equality & intersectionality

Recognising how disability, gender, age, poverty and social context overlap to shape risks, access and opportunities.

Humanitarian action

Frontline operational experience in Cox’s Bazar, with attention to service access, registration, information, referrals and respectful engagement.

Youth participation

Supporting youth voice through collaborative platforms, dialogue, advocacy and opportunities to influence decisions that affect communities.

Climate, ecosystems & resilience

An area of growing advocacy and learning: connecting inclusion with climate resilience, disaster preparedness and the protection of the natural environments communities depend on.

03 / Practice & experience

From community initiatives to humanitarian operations.

Humanitarian health operations · Cox’s Bazar

In my work with the International Rescue Committee in Ukhiya, I have supported health-facility operations through patient registration, records and data entry, reporting, patient flow, referral coordination and information support. This frontline work has strengthened my understanding of how administrative systems affect people’s access to services.

Child rights & community engagement

My grassroots engagement has included awareness activities related to child marriage, child labour and violence against children, working alongside young people, community members and local initiatives.

Youth and international platforms

I have contributed to youth participation and advocacy spaces, including Plan International Bangladesh’s Youth Advisory Panel and the Girls’ Pact / Summit of the Future Task Force, bringing community perspectives into wider conversations.

Gender equality, diversity & inclusion

Through youth, community and organisational platforms, I have engaged with gender-based violence prevention, sexual and reproductive health and rights, disability inclusion and more equitable participation.
