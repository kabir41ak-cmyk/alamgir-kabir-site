01 / Professional profile

Local roots. Wider perspective.

My work is grounded in the belief that meaningful development starts by listening to communities, recognizing people’s agency and building responses that are inclusive, accountable and practical.

What I bring

Community mobilization, humanitarian coordination, youth participation, advocacy, event-based programming and partnership development — with an emphasis on dignity, equity and measurable community value.

How I work

People-centred, collaborative and action-oriented. I connect grassroots realities with institutional priorities and communicate impact in a way that partners, funders and communities can understand.

03 / Field engagement

Action shaped around real community needs.

Eid clothing distribution

A recurring seasonal initiative supporting children and families to celebrate Eid with dignity. Across four years, the programme reached approximately 5,000 children.

Flood response · Feni & Jamalpur

Humanitarian relief and family-level distributions responding to flood-affected communities, with approximately 8,400 family distributions reported across the activities.

Winter support

Seasonal clothing support for people facing hardship, helping address immediate needs during colder months.

Youth, peace & civic engagement

Engagement through youth platforms, public dialogue, speaking opportunities and advocacy connected to inclusive development and social cohesion.
